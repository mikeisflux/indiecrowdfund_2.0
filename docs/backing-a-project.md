# Backing a Project

## How to Back

1. **Find a project** on the Discover page
2. **Click "Back this project"** on the project page
3. **Select a reward tier** (or pledge without a reward)
4. **Add optional add-ons** if available
5. **Enter payment details** and submit

## Payment Flow

**Your card is NOT charged immediately.**

- When you pledge, your card is saved using Stripe's SetupIntent
- Your card is only charged when the campaign reaches its funding goal
- If the campaign doesn't reach its goal, you're never charged

### What happens after funding?

| Campaign Status | What Happens |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Goal reached | Your card is charged automatically |
| Goal not reached | No charge - pledge expires |

## Managing Your Pledge

Before funding:
- Cancel your pledge anytime
- Increase your pledge amount

After funding:
- Add to your pledge
- Contact creator for refund requests

## FAQ

**When am I charged?**
Only when the campaign reaches its funding goal.

**Can I cancel?**
Yes, anytime before the campaign funds.

**What if the project fails?**
You're never charged if funding fails.
