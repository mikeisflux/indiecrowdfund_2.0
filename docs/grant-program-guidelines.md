# Divinity Comics Grant Program — Program Guidelines (Internal)

> **Status: DRAFT for attorney review.** This is the internal policy document for the

> nonprofit. It is the primary reference for how the program operates and should be
> reviewed and finalized with counsel before being adopted by the board.

## 1. Purpose

The Divinity Comics Grant Program (the "Program") is a formal grantmaking program of
IndieCrowdfund (a DBA of Divinity Comics Inc.) (the "Organization"). Its purpose is to further the
Organization's charitable mission of **promoting western comics and art** by awarding
grants that fund the creation and promotion of comics and related artistic projects by
independent creators.

## 2. How the Program Operates

1. Creators propose projects by submitting campaigns on IndieCrowdfund.com.
2. Supporters ("backers") contribute to the Organization through the platform's payment
   processors. Contributions are contributions to the Program.
3. The Organization retains a disclosed percentage of contributions to cover the
   reasonable administrative and facilitation costs of operating the Program (Section 5).
4. The remaining funds are awarded to the creator as a grant, under a signed Grant
   Agreement, to make the project described in the campaign.
5. After the grant is made, the creator is responsible for the project, including any
   rewards the creator chose to offer backers.

## 3. Eligibility

Projects are eligible for the Program when they:
- Further the Program's mission of promoting western comics and art (including the full
  spectrum of sequential art and related creative work);
- Comply with the platform's Terms of Service and Content Guidelines;
- Are proposed by a creator who has completed the platform's verification and due
  diligence process (identity verification, business filing, cross-platform fulfillment
  history review).

Purchases from the platform's Digital Shop and marketplace are ordinary commercial
transactions and are **not** part of the Program.

## 4. Selection & Discretion

- Every proposed campaign is reviewed by the Organization's admin team before launch.
  Review outcomes (approval, rejection, requested changes) and reviewer notes are
  recorded.
- Approval of a campaign admits the project to the Program; disbursement of funds
  constitutes the grant award.
- **Grants are awarded at the Organization's sole discretion**, even where projects are
  creator-initiated. The Organization may decline, withhold, or claw back a grant where a
  campaign violates platform policies or applicable law.

## 5. Administrative & Facilitation Costs

The Organization retains a disclosed percentage of contributions to cover the reasonable
costs of operating the Program, including: campaign review and creator due diligence,
payment processing and contribution handling, platform infrastructure and tooling
(surveys, fulfillment tooling, communications), record keeping, and program
administration. Payment-processor charges are passed through at cost. The retained
percentage is disclosed to creators during project setup and published on the
platform's fees page.

## 6. Grant Agreements

Every grantee signs the Grant Agreement (published at /terms?tab=grant) before funds are
disbursed. The agreement records: that funds are a grant from the Organization for the
project described; that the creator must use funds for that project; that rewards and
fulfillment are solely the creator's responsibility, including any tax consequences; and
the Organization's discretion over awards. Acceptances are recorded with the agreement
version, signer, project, and timestamp.

## 7. Record Keeping

The Organization maintains, for each grant:
- The campaign submission and admin review record (including reviewer notes on approval);
- The signed Grant Agreement (version + timestamp);
- Contribution totals, amounts retained for program costs, and the grant amount
  disbursed (exportable via the admin Grant Records report);
- Disbursement records (payout status and dates).

These records demonstrate that Program activity furthers the Organization's mission.

## 8. Review of These Guidelines

These guidelines are reviewed at least annually by the Organization's board, and any
material changes to the Program (retained percentage, eligibility, agreement terms) are
adopted before taking effect on the platform.
